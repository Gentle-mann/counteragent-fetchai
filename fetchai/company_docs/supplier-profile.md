# Fictional analytics software supplier

This agent represents a fictional company for the CounterAgent hackathon demo. It does not sell a real SaaS product or accept real payments.

Supported scope: analytics software for 250 employees, a 24-month subscription, annual USD pricing, EU processing, and negotiated company policy requirements.

Public interoperability: CounterAgentSupplierNegotiation version 1.0.0. Send a CapabilityRequest with a fresh request_id and nonce, category analytics_software, seats 250, term_months 24 and currency USD. Verify the returned CapabilityResponse signature against the discovered agent address. The signature proves address control and binds the supported scope; it does not prove real corporate authority.

Compatible buyers may send the published Rfp model and receive a Proposal with structured annual pricing and terms. Counteroffers are bounded to two rounds. Private reservation prices are never shared. Existing CounterAgent fictional treasury and executive agents handle demo-only closing; independent buyers must arrange their own evidence, authority and payment onboarding.

Searchable capabilities: analytics software supplier, enterprise analytics, procurement negotiation, signed capability discovery, annual subscription quote.
